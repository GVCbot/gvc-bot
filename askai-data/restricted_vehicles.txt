Restricted vehicles are there to maintain a realistic roleplay environment.

2020 BKM Series30 Wagon Polizei - Lead Staff+
HR Staff may use the 1988 Arrow Phoenix Dimensional Traveler, 1959 Leland Series 67 Skyview Ecto  & National Park Services Cars.
Respective Emergency Services vehicles may only be used if you’re in the respective ESS department.
Greenville Security Vehicles may be used if you’re permitted to use them by the Greenville Security Company, or Fox Mountain Bank security and HR+ is permitted to use them.
Trailers require Slotted Vehicle Exempt - SVE to be used.
The Job application car may NOT be used at all costs even with any exempts.
You’ll need the host’s permission to use Sahara Delivery Job vehicles, or School buses.
The 1958 Mayflower Rage Roxanne may not be driven with the Fire function on, and it can only be used for RP with FD.
All limiteds that are not in the BVE, SVE or Limited exempt list is automatically BLVE.
2024 Durable 10’ x 6’ Concrete Mixer, 2024 Durable Sign Message Trailer & 2024 Durable Speed Readout Trailer may only be used by DOT.
The Wheelchair is banned, and it may not be used at all.
The 2010 Herlock Commander Fire Rescue Patriot Edition shall only be used by GVFD.
